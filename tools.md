# Tools for YouTube creators

Tools can support planning and analysis, but they cannot replace a useful video or guarantee channel growth.

## 1. YouTube Studio

**What it is:** YouTube's own creator dashboard.

**Useful for:** Publishing videos, managing a channel, and reviewing available performance analytics.

[Open YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/)

## 2. vidIQ

**What it is:** A third-party service offering YouTube creator tools. Features and plan limits may change.

**Explore:** [vidIQ official website](https://vidiq.com/)

Before paying for a tool:
- Check its current features and pricing.
- See whether the features fit your workflow.
- Read its privacy policy and terms.
- Compare its suggestions with your own research.
- Do not treat scores or estimates as official YouTube ranking factors.

## 3. A simple no-cost workflow

- Brainstorm topics from audience questions.
- Check YouTube search suggestions.
- Plan a clear title and thumbnail.
- Publish and review YouTube Studio analytics.
- Record what you learn for the next video.
