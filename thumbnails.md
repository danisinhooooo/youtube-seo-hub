# YouTube thumbnails

A thumbnail gives viewers a visual preview of a video. It should help them understand the topic and should match what the video actually contains.

## Principles for a clear thumbnail

1. **One main idea:** avoid trying to show every detail.
2. **Readable at small sizes:** use a clear focal point and limited text.
3. **Strong contrast:** make important elements easy to distinguish.
4. **Consistent style:** a recognizable visual style can help viewers identify your videos.
5. **Honest preview:** do not show something that is not in the video.

## A simple planning checklist

- What is the video's main takeaway?
- What image best represents it?
- Is any text short and readable?
- Does the thumbnail work at a small size?
- Does it accurately represent the video?
- Are all images and fonts used with appropriate permission?

## Thumbnail and title should work together

The title can explain the topic while the thumbnail provides a visual cue. Avoid making them repeat exactly the same words.

## Accessibility and copyright

Use readable text and sufficient contrast. Use your own images or assets that you have permission to use. Check the licence for stock images, icons, and fonts before publishing.

For current technical requirements, consult [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/).
