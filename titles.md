# Writing useful YouTube titles

A good title helps the right viewer understand what a video offers. It should be accurate, specific, and easy to understand.

## A simple title checklist

- Does it describe the actual video?
- Is the main topic clear?
- Does it set a realistic expectation?
- Can someone understand it quickly?
- Does it avoid exaggerated or misleading promises?

## Helpful title patterns

Use these as starting points, not rigid formulas:

- **How to [do a task]** — How to Organize Your First Photo Shoot
- **[Topic] Explained** — Camera Aperture Explained for Beginners
- **[Number] Ways to [achieve a relevant outcome]** — 5 Ways to Improve Indoor Photos
- **Beginner's Guide to [topic]** — A Beginner's Guide to Manual Camera Settings
- **[A] vs [B]** — Prime Lens vs Zoom Lens: What's the Difference?

## Before and after examples

**Too vague:** My New Camera Video

**Clearer:** 5 Camera Settings Every Beginner Should Understand

**Misleading:** This Secret Guarantees You Millions of Views

**More honest:** 7 YouTube SEO Basics New Creators Can Learn

## Avoid

- ALL-CAPS titles that are hard to read.
- Repeating the same keyword unnaturally.
- Claims the video cannot support.
- Implying an endorsement or result that does not exist.

## Test thoughtfully

If you change a title, note the date and what changed. Review analytics over a reasonable period and consider other factors, such as traffic source and audience. Do not assume one change alone caused a result.
